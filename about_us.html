About Us

Mission: To raise awareness about substance abuse among teenagers aged 12-18 in Canada.

Substance abuse is a serious issue that affects many teenagers across the world. Drugs and alcohol can have a significant impact on a young person's life, causing long-term damage to their health and wellbeing.

If you are struggling with substance abuse, it's important to know that you are not alone. There are many resources available to help you overcome your addiction and get your life back on track.

To find out more inoformation, click here to visit our resources page.

On this website, you can find information about the different types of drugs and their effects, how to recognize the signs of substance abuse, and the risks and consequences of addiction.

We also provide tips on how to cope with peer pressure and how to seek help if you or someone you know is struggling with addiction.

Remember, seeking help is the first step towards recovery. There are many treatment options available, including counseling, support groups, and rehabilitation programs.

With the right support and guidance, you can overcome addiction and live a healthy, happy life.
