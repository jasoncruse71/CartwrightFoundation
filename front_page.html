Welcome to the Cartwright Foundation

Hi, my name is Jenna Cartwright.

The main purpose of this webiste is to increase awareness on teenage drug abuse, and help those in need.

I'd like to include a personal part of my life, in order to give meaning to the main objective of this website. I recently lost my daughter to drug abuse, and my heart aches for the parent's, and kid's who are victims of this fatal addiction.

If you've found yourself on this webiste, please do not hestitate to reach out to me, you can find my contact details on our 'contact' page. Teenage drug abuse has been on the rise in Canada in the recent years. Together, let's put an end to teenage drug abuse, and save our future generation.
